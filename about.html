About Featherston Consulting Group

Driven by data. Built on values. Focused on results.

Our Story

Featherston Consulting Group originated in Maryland and was founded by Javion Featherston, a United States Marine Corps veteran with a strong belief in innovation through data, technology, and operations management. The company was created to help small businesses overcome challenges and grow through structured problem-solving and strategic insight.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help businesses solve problems, improve operations, and grow through data-driven insights and practical consulting solutions.

Our Vision

We aim to grow Featherston Consulting Group into a trusted consulting firm that supports businesses across the country and eventually worldwide by delivering valuable insights, innovative strategies, and measurable results.
